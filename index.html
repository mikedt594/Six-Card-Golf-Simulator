Tips and Tricks

Stock is often strong early.You get to see a new card and can pass bad draws, so a merely okay discard is not always worth taking.
Take discard for clear value.Good discard takes usually lower shown score, make a column pair, or grab a premium low card.
Column cancels are huge.Matching ranks in a column score zero, so pairing a high card can beat simply lowering one slot.
2s and Kings are premium.2s score -2 and Kings score 0, so they are usually worth protecting or taking.
Do not overpay for denial.Blocking an opponent matters, but raising your own score needs a very clear reason.
Close EVs are not commandments.If options are within a fraction of a point, table context and discard history can decide it.
